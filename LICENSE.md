## What a commercial licence actually buys

Plainly, because the code is readable and we would rather you know what you are
paying for:

1. **The standards corpus.** `ace-standards` ships no corpus. It reads one you
   configure, and that corpus is separately licensed, maintained and re-ingested
   by AceEngineer. Without it the agent resolves nothing and says so.
2. **Maintenance.** Edition corrections, corpus updates, skill repointing when
   upstream libraries move — the things that decay fastest and silently.
3. **Support**, and the right to rely on the verification gate in work you put
   your professional name to.

A licence that lapses does not disable this software. It stops the corpus
updates and the edition corrections, which is what makes it stale — and stale is
the failure mode that matters in standards work.
